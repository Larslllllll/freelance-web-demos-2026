# Prospect audit — Hotz Gärtnerei AG

Source reviewed: https://hotz-gaertnerei.ch/ (public pages, 2026-09-19)

## Observed opportunity
- The business has announced a major change: the retail shop closes on 11 July 2026, while services, subscriptions, weekly market and business customers continue.
- That change deserves a focused landing page so visitors do not mistake the closure for the end of the business.
- The demo makes the new offer and contact path visible immediately.

## Demo changes
- Prominent notice for the shop closure and continuing services.
- Four clear service categories.
- Focus on plants, subscriptions and business service instead of a traditional shop.
- No invented claims or customer reviews.

Private sales demo; approval is required before publishing or using brand assets.
