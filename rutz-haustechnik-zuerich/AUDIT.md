# Prospect audit — Rutz & Co AG

Source reviewed: https://www.rutz-sanitaer-heizung.ch/ (public pages, 2026-09-19)

## Observed opportunity
- The existing site has strong factual content and SEO pages, but the navigation and repeated sections make the primary paths (offer, emergency, service) compete for attention.
- A conversion-first front page can put emergency contact, 90+ years of trust, core services and offer request into a shorter decision path.
- The demo preserves only public claims: Zürich/Limmattal, 90+ years, services, phone and email.

## Demo changes
- Clear water/warmth positioning above the fold.
- Four high-signal service cards with emergency CTA.
- Trust strip and one offer-request path.
- No invented reviews, project photos or guarantees.

Private sales demo; approval is required before publishing or using brand assets.
