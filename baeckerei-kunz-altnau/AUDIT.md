# Prospect audit — Bäckerei Kunz GmbH

Public source: https://www.baeckereikunz.ch/ (reviewed 2026-09-19)

## Observed opportunity
- The current site contains strong offers across several locations: products, tortes, weekly lunch menus and catering.
- A focused campaign page can turn this breadth into one clear catering and event inquiry path.
- Public facts used: Altnau base, Altnau/Uttwil/Arbon locations, self-made products, lunch menus, tortes, catering, telephone and public email.

## Demo changes
- One clear message for private and business events.
- Catering gets its own high-visibility section and CTA.
- Weekly menu and take-away offer appear above the fold.
- No invented reviews or guarantees.
