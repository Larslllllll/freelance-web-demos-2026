# Prospect audit — Bäckerei + Konditorei Hackebeil

Public source: https://www.baeckerei-hackebeil.ch/ (reviewed 2026-09-19)

## Observed opportunity
- The public site is a React application with useful facts, but its initial HTML is effectively empty until JavaScript runs.
- A fast, server-rendered fallback or focused landing page can make the morning offer, opening times and call action immediately legible.
- Public facts used: Arbon, since 1998, phone +41 (0)71 446 10 83, opening hours, address and public email.

## Demo changes
- Immediate above-the-fold call-to-action for today’s visit.
- Opening times and phone link are visible without hunting.
- Four product groups give the visitor a quick orientation.
- No invented awards, testimonials or product claims.

Private sales demo. Approval is required before publication or reuse of brand assets.
