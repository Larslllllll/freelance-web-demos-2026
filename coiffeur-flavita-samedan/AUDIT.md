# Prospect audit — Coiffeur Flavita, Samedan

Source reviewed: https://coiffeurflavita.ch/ (public pages, 2026-09-19)

## Observed opportunity
- Current site identifies as WordPress/Divi and still exposes a `Copyright © 2021` footer.
- The page contains strong personal story and pricing, but the opening section is text-heavy and the primary booking action can be made more prominent.
- A focused mobile-first layout can surface location, booking, trust and core services faster.

## Demo changes
- Rebuilt hero around one action: online booking.
- Grouped services into three understandable categories.
- Preserved only publicly stated facts and prices.
- No fabricated review, result, photo or credential.

This is a private sales demo, not an official site. Obtain approval before publishing or using branding/assets.
